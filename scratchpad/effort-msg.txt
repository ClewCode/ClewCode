fix: clamp /effort xhigh/max to high for OpenAI-compatible providers

Claude 4.6/4.7 accept xhigh/max as effort levels, but OpenAI reasoning_effort
only admits low|medium|high. getOpenAIReasoningEffort forwarded the raw string,
so opencode/deepseek/nvidia requests 400'd on /effort xhigh or max. Clamp
xhigh/max to high and skip unknown levels instead of forwarding them.
